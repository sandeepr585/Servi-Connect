# Servi-Connect

LINK : https://serviconnect-phi.vercel.app/
ServiConnect is a full-stack service marketplace that connects customers with trusted professionals for home services. Built with React and Spring Boot, featuring service discovery, bookings, cart, payments, provider management, admin dashboards, ratings, and real-time booking workflows.


# 🚀 ServiConnect

> A modern full-stack service marketplace that connects customers with trusted service professionals for everyday home and personal services.

ServiConnect is designed to make discovering, booking, managing, and tracking professional services simple and convenient.

Customers can browse services, compare professionals, add services to their cart, make bookings, track booking status, and provide ratings and reviews.

Service providers can manage their services, bookings, availability, customers, and earnings.

Administrators can manage users, services, providers, bookings, and the overall platform.
